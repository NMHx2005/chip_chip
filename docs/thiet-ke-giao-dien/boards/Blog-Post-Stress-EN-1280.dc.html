Why the ability to fabricate a leading-edge chip, not just to design one, has become one of the most closely watched capabilities in the global economy

Posted Sep 12, 2026

Every new generation of chips packs billions more transistors than the last, yet the phone in your pocket does not run as hot as a stove. That is no accident. It is the result of one engineering decision made again and again: lower the voltage.
